About the authors

BelemConverse is built by Marco Mollinetti and Artur Nakauth Freires.

Marco Mollinetti

AI Division Manager, Nohara Group (Japan)

Marco leads BelemConverse. He runs the AI division at the Nohara Group, a Japanese construction company in its fourth century. A team of three to five, three of them PhDs, carries nine concurrent projects across Japan, Singapore and Indonesia. They include a constraint-programming and vision engine for gypsum board nesting and an OCR pipeline that reads six required fields off handwritten delivery faxes. The division also covers the group's AI governance frameworks and its reskilling programmes.

He came to the job through research and engineering. He has a Ph.D. in Systems Engineering from the University of Tsukuba, on deterministic methods for non-deterministic optimisation, and did a postdoc at CENSIPAM on autonomous oil-spill and vessel detection. In production he worked at Plimes on cough and swallow recognition, and at MTI on container scheduling across Japan, Vietnam and Singapore and on rain forecasting for aviation maps. As a research fellow at Lab-TeC he won a $200,000 CNPq grant to study lightning detection in the Amazon, with UFPA and the University of Arizona.

He took his bachelor's degree at the University Centre of Pará, finished first in his class, and still gives guest lectures on applied AI and optimisation at CESUPA. He knows the city this system indexes.

Artur Nakauth Freires

Cybersecurity, Chattahoochee Technical College (USA)

Artur is studying cybersecurity at Chattahoochee Technical College in Georgia, where he holds a 4.0 GPA, is treasurer of the National Technical Honor Society and has spoken as a guest in two of the college's CIST courses. He got into security through data analytics (the Google Data Analytics certificate, then volunteer analysis work for the GLOCAL Foundation of Canada), and into data through games.

Games took him to Vancouver, where he earned a diploma in game design at Vancouver Film School and then worked in QA at The Coalition, the Xbox studio behind Gears of War. There he was point of contact for the Map Builder feature and ran FPS, CPU and GPU performance testing. At EA Burnaby, through Keywords Studios, he became an area lead, running a team of more than twenty testers across three production cycles and working with producers and developers on scoping, risk tracking and play sessions.

He started in Belém. He took his computer science degree at CESUPA and published his first AI work there in 2012: a multilayer perceptron for movie ranking at the SBPC annual meeting, and AmAIzed, a tool for teaching artificial intelligence in high schools, at CNMAC. In the same years he co-founded HertzOne, a Belém esports organisation that ran League of Legends and Hearthstone tournaments for four years. Most recently he managed contracts and budgets for a hotel in Castanhal, east of the city, the kind of local business BelemConverse exists to point visitors toward.
